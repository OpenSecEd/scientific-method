## Checks made on Asta's citations

- All 41 links resolve to a Semantic Scholar record whose title is the one
  the report gives.
- Five quoted passages were compared with the full texts of three sources
  (Klivan et al. 2023, arXiv version; Ngandu et al. 2025; Husak et al. 2016)
  and found word for word.
- Four sources are cited for a claim that the passage shown from them does
  not contain, while another quoted source cites them for it: Mohammed et
  al. 2019 (via Ubagaram et al.), Sarkar et al. 2023 and Shahidinejad et al.
  2021 (via Fathima et al.), Husak et al. 2016 (via Mooghala). The full text
  of Husak et al. contains neither "multi-factor", "two-factor" nor
  "man-in-the-middle".
- The labels "(Model-Generated)" (4) and "(LLM Memory)" (1) occur five
  times, all but one in the closing section.
