<p>You can pass this course through two paths:</p>
<ol style="list-style-type: decimal;">
<li>By participating in all teaching. You have to do all prep assignments and actively participate in the discussions during the seminars.</li>
<li>You do the project in the end. During the project you must show that you have learned everything that was covered during the course. You must write a report, peer-review another group's report, present and actively participate in discussions during the final seminar.</li>
</ol>
<h3>How to pass by participation</h3>
<ul>
<li>You must do all FeedbackFruits assignments. You must get at least 90/100 on all of them. (Not the TAMOS video assignments.)</li>
<li>You must submit all reflection assignments.</li>
<li><strong>Do this before each seminar.</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>You can see a complete list if you open <a title="Assignments" href="$CANVAS_COURSE_REFERENCE$/assignments" data-course-type="navigation" data-api-endpoint="$CANVAS_COURSE_REFERENCE$/assignments" data-api-returntype="[Assignment]">Assignments</a> and select "By type" in the top right corner (default is "By date", which sorts by due date).</p>

<p>Everything under "Participation INL1" must be done.</p>
<p><strong>Once that's done, you get Ps on all assignments in INL1 except <a title="Course evaluation survey" href="$CANVAS_COURSE_REFERENCE$/assignments/Course%20evaluation%20survey" data-course-type="assignments" data-published="true" data-api-endpoint="$CANVAS_COURSE_REFERENCE$/assignments/Course%20evaluation%20survey" data-api-returntype="Assignment">Course evaluation survey</a>,</strong> which you must complete once it opens the last week of the course.</p>
<p><strong>If you aim for this, DO NOT OPEN any of the assignments under INL1.</strong></p>
<h3>How to get a grade</h3>
<p>You must receive passing grades on all assignments under "INL1". Either by participation (above) or doing them.</p>

<p><strong>For those who must do these assignments: </strong>Note that these assignments have hard deadlines. E.g. if you miss the peer review, you can't do the seminar. If you can't do the seminar, you'll have to do it the next time the course is given.</p>
