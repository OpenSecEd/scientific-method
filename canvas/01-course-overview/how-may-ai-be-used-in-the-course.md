Generative AI tools may be used in all examination and graded
assignments as long as the use follows the course guidelines, unless
otherwise explicitly stated. You are also advised to follow the course
guidelines for non-graded assignments. The guidelines for the use of
generative AI are designed to promote learning and the fulfillment of
the course learning objectives.

## Guidelines for the use of generative AI

Generative AI may be used in the course\'s examination and graded
assignments as long as you use it in a manner that is

- responsible
- ethical
- academically honest.
- [that doesn\'t solve the assignment for
  you.]{style="background-color: #fbeeb8;"}

One example on how to fulfill these guidelines is to **be transparent
with your use of generative AI,** which means that your use must be
disclosed. You will find more course-specific examples under the heading
\"Examples of permitted use\". 

Note that the guidelines must be followed for examination and graded
assignments, but you are advised to also follow them outside of
examinations and for non-graded assignments.

### Examples of permitted use

Below are examples of permitted use of generative AI in the course.
Consult your teacher if you are considering other areas of use.

- [**Idea generation:** Use AI to get inspiration, structure thoughts or
  formulate questions. However, it is always good to think independently
  first, so that the AI ​​tool does not limit your
  perspectives.]{style="background-color: #fbeeb8;"}
- [**Reviewing your work:** Let the AI review and give feedback on your
  work. For instance, on language and grammar. It can also review other
  aspects, but be aware that the AI might be wrong when it comes to
  subject matter (very likely).]{style="background-color: #fbeeb8;"}
- [**Using the AI tools taught in the course:** We teach a few AI-based
  tools in the course, those may be used where suitable. Note that it is
  part of the assessment to be able to determine when it is suitable or
  not.]{style="background-color: #fbeeb8;"}
- [**Discussing course content:** You may discuss course content with
  AI, e.g. summarize notes or comparing two research papers. **However,
  be aware that the AI might hallucinate.** Always use it for tasks that
  you can verify; for instance, you can verify similarities and
  differences between papers yourself after the AI has pointed them
  out\-\--but that requires you to read the papers yourself
  too!]{style="background-color: #fbeeb8;"}

**Note!** Remember to critically review material created by generative
AI. **You as a student are fully responsible** for all material you
submit and must be able to defend and explain it completely without
support from generative AI.

### Examples of prohibited use

Below is a description of the use of generative AI in examination and
graded assignments that is considered unauthorized assistance, attempted
deception or cheating. This is **not** a complete list, always consult
your teacher if you are unsure whether your use is permitted.

- [**Assignment solution:** AI may not be used to generate solutions to
  entire assignments, reports, reflection answers or parts
  thereof.]{style="background-color: #fbeeb8;"}
- [**AI to improve solution:** You may not ask AI how to fix the
  comments in the feedback that you got from a teacher, that would be
  asking it to solve the assignment. But you are allowed to ask it how
  to better highlight the parts that you already have, that you think
  that the teacher didn\'t see. (The latter is about presentation of
  *your* solution, not creating a
  solution.)]{style="background-color: #fbeeb8;"}

 

## Disclosure of AI use

To ensure academic transparency, you must disclose whether and how
generative AI has been used in your work. The disclosure should be made
as follows: [Add comments that points out where and how AI has been
used, for example:]{style="background-color: #fbeeb8;"}

- [Add the text "Image created using AI tool 1" to the caption of a
  figure in a report.]{style="background-color: #fbeeb8;"}
- [Add a footnote to a section heading stating that "This section has
  been formatted using AI tool 2 with the following
  prompt:\...".]{style="background-color: #fbeeb8;"}

 

## Disciplinary action for unauthorized use of AI

Everything you submit must be your own work -- even if generative AI is
used as support, the thinking and conclusions must be your own. Using
generative AI in an unauthorized way for examination and graded
assignments is considered an attempt to mislead and **may lead to
disciplinary action**. Therefore, be sure to follow the information on
this page, and disclose your use of generative AI.

 

## Questions or concerns?

Contact the course responsible for guidance and clarification of this
information, or if you are wrongly accused of unauthorized use of
generative AI in the course\'s examination and graded assignments.
