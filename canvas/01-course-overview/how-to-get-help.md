## Any time

Use the
[Discussions]($CANVAS_COURSE_REFERENCE$/discussion_topics "Discussions"){api-endpoint="$CANVAS_COURSE_REFERENCE$/discussion_topics"
api-returntype="[Discussion]"} feature. Post your question and you will
eventually get an answer. If your question is related to some difficulty
understanding part of the material, just ask at that location in the
material.

However, this is **limited to the period when the course is running.**
If you don\'t make it, register for the course again the next time it is
given (ask student-support@eecs.kth.se) so that you get access to the
current Canvas room.
