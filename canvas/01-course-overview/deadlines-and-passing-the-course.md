- do all prep and actively participate in all seminars, **or**
- complete [The final
  assignment]($CANVAS_COURSE_REFERENCE$/modules/The%20final%20assignment%20%28week%2050-1%29 "The final assignment"){course-type="modules"

## The final assignment

This is the seminar in the exam period and its preparation assignment,
everything in the module [The final
assignment]($CANVAS_COURSE_REFERENCE$/modules/The%20final%20assignment%20%28week%2050-1%29 "The final assignment"){course-type="modules"
published="false"
api-endpoint="$CANVAS_COURSE_REFERENCE$/modules/The%20final%20assignment%20%28week%2050-1%29"
api-returntype="Module"}. The preparation will be peer reviewed;
consequently, you must make the deadline to participate. **If you don\'t
hand in on time, you\'ll have to wait until the next round of seminars
(in the next course round).** The person who will review your work must
get sufficient time to review it on time for the seminar.

The reviewer will present the review at the seminar, so that\'s the
deadline. **If the reviewer doesn\'t make it on time**, the reviewer
will have to review someone else and present during the next round of
seminars (in the next period).

 

## The other seminars and prep assignments

**The other assignments in the course are learning material.** (That is
all other modules in the course.) They are preparation for formative
seminars to help you learn. However, if you don\'t do the work before
the seminar, you will probably not get much out of the seminar.

Now, I have adapted these assignments and the seminars so that **if you
do all learning assignments on time (before the respective seminars) and
actively participate in these seminars (I must be able to assess you on
the intended learning outcomes)**, you don\'t have to do the final
assignment.

If you miss a seminar and still don\'t want to do the project, you can
just do that seminar in the next course round. To do that, you must have
access to that round\'s Canvas. Email
[student-support@eecs.kth.se](mailto:student-support@eecs.kth.se){target="_blank"}
to get help with that, they\'ll instruct you with what you must do.
